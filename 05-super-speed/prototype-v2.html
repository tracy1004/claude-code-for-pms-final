Meteor Mite's phone (app 4.3)

  1. The phone opens on a 4.3 update notification. Tap it, read the log, tick the box and press Acknowledge. You can't skip it, but a live ping always comes first.
  2. Press Send a ping. The wait is 75 seconds. The phone vibrates (a real vibration on an Android phone; on a computer the phone shakes) and repeats every 15 seconds.
  3. Try Decline then Undo, or let it time out and claim it.
  4. Press Plan time away: pick when it starts and how many days. Then check the Handler console: it shows on the planned-time-off board.
  5. While a ping is live, press Need 30 more seconds (once per ping) or Emergency (asks you to confirm; a ping passed on for an emergency does not count against you).
  6. After you take a callout you are On a job. Press Mark job complete to go back to available.
  7. Try Break, Lunch and Emergency. On the console, an emergency shows a red banner with an Acknowledge button; the phone then shows that the handler has seen it.
  8. On the console, open a missed ping: Update this missed ping (a written reason is required) or Resend it (only if the responder is within 15 minutes; Meteor Mite is 9, The Gale is 22). Press Reset responder on a card too. Come back here to see the notification the responder gets.
