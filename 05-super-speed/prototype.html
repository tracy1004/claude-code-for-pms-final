Meteor Mite's phone

Shows P1, P2 and P4 from the brief. What you do here shows up on Kip's console.

  1. Press Send a ping. The 60-second wait starts.
  2. Press Decline. A 10-second Undo appears (P1).
  3. Or do nothing until it reaches 0. The ping stays reachable for a moment (P2) if nobody else has taken it.
  4. Then switch to Handler console to see it in the timeline.

Also shows P5: the "What we measure" screen, listing exactly what the app reports.

Wait is 60s here because that's the shipped 4.2 value. Whether it goes back to 90s is a decision for Helen and Wen Li (see the brief).
